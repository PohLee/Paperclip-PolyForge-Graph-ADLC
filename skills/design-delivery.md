# Design delivery

Use for a minor design change or a major direction and candidate study.

1. Read the versioned requirement, active product decisions, user research, and design constraints. Identify the branch chosen by the Graph.
2. For major work, produce two distinct wireframe directions after principle research. Supply anonymized evidence to the independent norms, Product Owner fit, and blind checks before direction promotion.
3. For each promoted direction, produce the Graph's candidate count with accessibility and edge states; freeze each candidate identity and request independent QC. Keep rejected versions and reasons.
4. Prototype two finalists in isolated executions only when the route calls for A/B. Record the comparison or a reasoned no-prototype record. Complete when the final design decision cites exact finalist and experiment versions.

An external design skill or editor may improve the work only after the host confirms installation and permission. State which checks remain unverified if the tool is unavailable.
