# CPO and CTO conflict resolution

Use when product and engineering owners disagree about a plan or acceptance.

1. Save both original positions, evidence, assumptions and non-negotiables. Compare scope, timing, resources, risk and success criteria even if verdict labels match.
2. Deduplicate conflicts. Try phased sequencing for priority, compatible constraints for scope, impact/likelihood/reversibility for risk and commitment evidence for resources. Ask each owner to restate a final position after a focused challenge.
3. Record convergence or the unresolved choice. When material disagreement remains, pause affected work and obtain an authenticated human decision before resuming. Complete only with a linked resolution record.
