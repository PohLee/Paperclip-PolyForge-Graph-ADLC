# CPO and CTO conflict resolution

Use when product and engineering owners disagree about a plan or acceptance.

1. Have the CPO and CTO + CAIO each record a signed position in their own current Graph task execution. State GO, MODIFY or BLOCK; scope, timeline, resources, risk and success criteria; non-negotiables; and sourced evidence. Retain both Decision IDs.
2. Deduplicate conflicts. Try phased sequencing for priority, compatible constraints for scope, impact/likelihood/reversibility for risk and commitment evidence for resources. Record at least one proposal and both owners' responses with evidence.
3. If disagreement remains, open a conflict using the two current position Decision IDs, negotiation record, unresolved issues and explicit human options. Give each option an executable disposition: continue, rework with an upstream target node, or stop. Pause affected work.
4. Complete when the authenticated human owner records a linked resolution and its disposition takes effect. A continued gate cites that resolution before deciding; rework produces a new target attempt; stop ends the run. A retry or rework invalidates a position tied to an older attempt.
