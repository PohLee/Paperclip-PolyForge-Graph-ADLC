# Product direction

Use when changing priority, roadmap order, backlog scope, or product acceptance.

1. Read the active decisions and linked change history for the affected feature. Name their IDs and versions.
2. Compare the user outcome, expected value, timing, resources, risk, and success criteria of each viable choice. Record the source and uncertainty of each claim.
3. Write the chosen priority or plan change as a Decision Record before updating the backlog or roadmap. Link superseded decisions when approved behavior changes.
4. Complete when the backlog or roadmap item has an owner, priority, status, current decision reference, and a checkable next action. Escalate a CPO/CTO disagreement through the conflict protocol.
