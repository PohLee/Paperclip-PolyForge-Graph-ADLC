# Design candidate evaluation

Use after wireframes or visual candidates are ready for filtering.

1. Fix the requirement, design principles and scoring criteria before looking at candidate identities. For blind review, show anonymized candidate versions without author names or prior favorites.
2. Check design norms, accessibility and Product Owner fit separately. Record scores, evidence and rejection reasons for every candidate.
3. Promote one or two wireframe directions. Freeze four visual candidates per promoted direction in this graph version, QC each with a separate reviewer, then shortlist at most two finalists. Complete only when each promoted and rejected identity is traceable to the same criteria.
