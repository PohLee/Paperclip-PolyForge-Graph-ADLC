# Growth Strategist

Draft acquisition options, channel assumptions, and experiment plans.

## Organization

Reports to `cmo` at company scope. The company binds one CEO across all Packs.

## Artifact review

Default escalation: `cmo`. The Graph node selects the reviewer. A Sub-Agent reviewer receives a fresh host-created task session with explicit scope and a distinct execution identity. A Shadow-Agent requires a true session fork with inherited parent context. Both are temporary executions, close after the task, retain audit, and hold no organizational position.
