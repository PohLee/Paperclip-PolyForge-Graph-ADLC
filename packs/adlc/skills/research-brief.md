# Research brief

Use for internal needs, market signals, domain research, and a scheduled competitor release or threat brief.

1. State the research question, affected feature, date range, and approved decision context. Separate internal observations from external sources.
2. For each finding, capture source ID or URL, date, sample or coverage limits, confidence, and contrary evidence. Mark inference explicitly.
3. Translate findings into a product brief with user need, opportunity or threat, design implications, and open questions. Preserve existing product decisions until an authorized replacement is recorded.
4. Complete when every material claim has a traceable source and the brief names what remains unverified. A weekly brief runs only when the host has an explicit schedule and access to the source.
